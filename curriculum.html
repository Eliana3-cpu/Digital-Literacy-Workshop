What Students Are Learning

Our workshops are designed to make technology approachable, engaging, and practical for students who may be encountering computers for the first time. Sessions are visual, interactive, and hands-on, taught primarily in Amharic with English used when helpful. The curriculum grows with our students as they move from understanding technology to confidently creating with it.

Digital Devices beginner

Building a foundation in computers and digital devices — understanding what they are, how their parts work together, and how to use them with confidence.

Internet & Search Skills beginner

Learning how to navigate the internet, find useful information, ask better questions, and explore the digital world independently.

Digital Safety safety

Understanding how to use technology responsibly, protect personal information, recognize online risks, and make safer digital choices.

Introductory Coding coding

Introducing the ideas behind coding through simple, creative activities that develop logical thinking, problem-solving, and curiosity.

Scratch coding

Using Scratch to turn ideas into animations, stories, and simple interactive projects while learning how code can bring those ideas to life.

Games & Animations coding

Applying digital and coding skills to create games, animations, and other projects that students can share and build on.
