Teaching kids in Ethiopia to use computers, one Zoom class at a time.

Growing from my own experiences with limited access to technology, this student-led initiative provides interactive digital literacy and introductory coding education to children in Ethiopia. Through weekly virtual workshops, partnerships with local organizations, and growing technology resources, we are helping students build practical skills, confidence, and curiosity for a digital world.

What We Do

We make technology education more accessible to children in Ethiopia through interactive, beginner-friendly virtual workshops. Students explore digital literacy, computer skills, online safety, and introductory coding through lessons designed around curiosity, creativity, and hands-on learning.

Students who once needed a coordinator’s help just to join a Zoom session eventually learned to navigate it independently and confidently. Moments like this remind me that digital literacy is not just about learning how to use technology — it is about building the confidence to use it independently.

Where We Stand

What began as an idea to share my knowledge is already becoming a growing community effort. We currently work with two partner organizations, reaching students through biweekly virtual workshops while developing bilingual learning materials, collecting technology books, and working toward expanding access to computers and other learning resources.
