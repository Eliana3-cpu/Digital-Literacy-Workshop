Teaching kids in Ethiopia to use computers, one Zoom class at a time.

I grew up without reliable electricity or internet. Now I run weekly digital-literacy and introductory-coding workshops — taught mostly in Amharic — for students ages 11 to 15 who are starting where I once did.

What we do

Students who often have little or no prior experience with computers learn how to use digital devices, navigate and search the internet safely, and get an introduction to coding through Scratch — building small games and animations along the way. Classes are visual, interactive, and hands-on rather than lecture-based.

One thing I'm proud of: students who used to need a coordinator's help just to open Zoom can now join our sessions completely on their own.

Where things stand right now

Workshops are running in partnership with two nonprofit organizations, including Ethiopia Reads. I'm currently collecting beginner-friendly technology books for a small lending library, and building out this website to document the curriculum and eventually make parts of it reusable by other educators.
