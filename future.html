Growing From Here

We are working toward building a sustainable model for technology education that can reach more students and more communities over time.

  • More Students Expand workshops through additional community organizations.
  • More Volunteers Build a team of volunteers who can teach, support students, and develop new learning materials.
  • More Resources Collect and distribute computers, technology books, and other educational resources.
  • Better Curriculum Develop a reusable, bilingual digital-literacy curriculum that partner organizations can continue using beyond individual workshops.
  • Student Creators Give students opportunities to move beyond learning about technology and begin creating with it.
