Why I Started This

My interest in technology began with something very simple: access. Growing up in Ethiopia, I experienced what it was like to have limited access to computers, reliable internet, and other technology. When I later moved to a school with greater access to computers and the internet, I saw how quickly technology could change the way I learned and explored the world.

That experience stayed with me. Years later, while volunteering with children in my community, I met students who had very limited opportunities to interact with technology. One experience in particular made me realize that access to technology is not only about having a device. It is also about knowing how to use it, understanding what is possible with it, and feeling confident enough to explore. I wanted to contribute the knowledge I had gained.That became the beginning of this initiative.

What started as an idea to share basic digital skills is growing into something larger: a student-led effort to make technology education more accessible, engaging, and sustainable for children in Ethiopia.
