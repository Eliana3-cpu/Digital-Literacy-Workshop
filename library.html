Building A Technology Library

Access to technology education is not limited to computers. We are also building a collection of technology and computer-related books that can give students opportunities to continue exploring outside of our virtual sessions. More than 20 books have already been collected, with plans to expand the collection and make these resources available to students through our partner organizations.
