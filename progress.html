Measuring More Than Attendance

Our goal is not simply to introduce students to technology, but to help them become confident, independent users and eventually creators. Student progress is tracked through participation, practical activities, knowledge checks, and hands-on projects as students move through the curriculum.

Starting Point

  • 96% of students have limited or no previous experience using computers.
  • Limited exposure to common digital devices and software.
  • Limited English proficiency can create an additional barrier to technology learning.

Learning Milestone

  • Identify common computer hardware and explain what different components do.
  • Distinguish between hardware and software.
  • Use basic computer functions with increasing independence.
  • Navigate basic digital tools and online resources.
  • Explain fundamental digital-safety practices.
    • Use simple problem-solving strategies when working with technology. Begin applying introductory coding concepts through Scratch and other interactive activities.
