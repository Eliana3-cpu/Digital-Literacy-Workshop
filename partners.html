Who We Work With

Ethiopia Reads

A grassroots education organization focused on expanding access to books, community libraries, teacher training, and literacy opportunities for children in Ethiopia. Through this partnership, the initiative extends that focus on educational access into digital literacy and technology skills.

Samaritan Health And Education Support Center

A community-focused organization supporting children and families through education and other essential services. Through this partnership, the initiative brings structured digital-literacy instruction directly to students with limited prior exposure to computers and technology.
